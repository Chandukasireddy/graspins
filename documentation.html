How we work with you.

A practical guide to engaging Graspins — our process, the technologies we use, and what to expect at each stage. For hands-on help, see our support page.

Our engagement process

Every engagement follows four clear stages — architect, develop, deploy, and maintain. We start with a short discovery to understand your goals and data, produce a proposed architecture and plan, then build in tight iterations with regular demos.

  • Discovery & scoping — goals, data audit, success metrics, and feasibility.
  • Architecture — system design, model approach, and a phased delivery plan.
  • Build & evaluate — iterative development with measurable evaluation at each step.
  • Deploy — production rollout with monitoring, guardrails, and documentation.
  • Maintain — ongoing monitoring, retraining, and improvements.

Technologies we use

We're pragmatic about tools and choose what fits the problem. Typical stacks include Python with PyTorch, TensorFlow, and scikit-learn for modeling; modern LLM providers and open-weight models for generative AI; vector databases for retrieval; and cloud platforms (AWS, GCP, Azure) with containerized, observable deployments.

Integration & ownership

We integrate with your existing systems through clean APIs and well-documented code, and we hand over everything you need to own the solution: source code, infrastructure-as-code, runbooks, and clear documentation. You're never locked in.

Security & data handling

We treat your data with care — least-privilege access, encryption in transit and at rest, and clear data-handling agreements. We design AI systems with safety, privacy, and governance in mind from day one.
